I’ve always learned best by making things. The harder part has been slowing down long enough to record what happened: what worked, what failed, and what I understand now that I didn’t understand when I started.

This site is a return to that habit.

## What I’m working on

Right now, most of my energy is going into two products:

- **Cyberdeck**, a keyboard-first command center for working with multiple local coding agents.
- **ClimbSite**, a trip-planning tool that connects climbing areas with camping, approaches, parking, and the other details that shape a weekend outside.

They’re very different products, but both are teaching me how to turn an idea into something useful—and how AI changes the way that work gets done.

## What belongs here

These field notes won’t all be polished essays. Some will be build logs, short lessons, decisions I want to remember, or experiments that didn’t work. Others will become deeper case studies as the projects mature.

The goal is simple: document the progress while it’s happening, make my thinking visible, and build a more honest record of the work.

There’s more to come.
